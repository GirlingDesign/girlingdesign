Corporate Building Services

Maintenance and project support for offices and professional workplaces.

Corporate building services

Corporate environments need building work to be well coordinated, clearly communicated and managed with minimal disruption to day-to-day operations.

AssetTFM supports offices and professional environments with maintenance, repairs, fit-outs, refurbishments, reporting and project works.

Workplace services

Our trade services can support one-off works, ongoing maintenance and larger projects. Services include, but are not limited to:

  • General maintenance and repairs
  • Office fit-outs
  • Painting and plastering
  • Electrical and plumbing coordination
  • Carpentry and cabinetry
  • Flooring
  • Tiling and glazing
  • Signage installation
  • Window frosting and blinds
  • Furniture-related works
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Refurbishment works

Reporting

AssetTFM can provide building inspections and practical reporting to help identify defects, maintenance priorities and longer-term property requirements.

Project management

For planned workplace changes, AssetTFM can coordinate scope, contractors, site activity, documentation and final handover through one point of contact.

Planning maintenance or workplace works?

Use Service Enquiry for maintenance and smaller requirements, or Project Enquiry for planned fit-outs, refurbishments and larger works.
