We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information in a clear and responsible way. This Privacy Policy explains the types of personal information we may collect through this website, why we collect it, how it may be used and how you can contact us about your information.

This policy should be read together with any additional privacy notice shown when information is collected.

1. Information we may collect

When you contact AssetTFM through this website, we may collect information such as:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your company name, if you choose to provide it
  • whether you are contacting us as a client or contractor
  • the service sector relevant to your enquiry
  • the address of works for a service enquiry
  • whether you have set a budget
  • a project budget range for a project enquiry
  • the status of any plans
  • preferred attendance dates or time windows
  • information you include in your enquiry or project description
  • other information you choose to provide to us

We do not ask for payment card information through this website.

The website enquiry forms do not accept file uploads.

2. Why we collect personal information

We collect personal information so that we can:

  • respond to enquiries
  • understand the work or service you are asking about
  • assess and prepare for service requests or project discussions
  • provide quotes or further information
  • communicate with you about your enquiry
  • manage our business and client relationships
  • meet legal, regulatory or record-keeping obligations where applicable

We will only use personal information for purposes reasonably connected with the reason it was collected, or as otherwise permitted or required by law.

3. How information is collected

We may collect personal information when you:

  • submit a General Enquiry form
  • submit a Service Enquiry form
  • submit a Project Enquiry form
  • communicate with us after making an enquiry
  • provide documents or images through an external file request link

We may also receive ordinary technical information generated when a website is accessed, such as server logs required for security and site operation.

6. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:

  • employees, contractors or service providers involved in responding to your enquiry or carrying out work
  • professional advisers where required
  • technology providers that support our website, email, forms or document transfer
  • regulators, authorities or other parties where required or authorised by law

We do not sell personal information.

7. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Personal information may be stored in email systems, business records and systems used to manage enquiries and work.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If AssetTFM becomes aware of a data incident, it will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements and its circumstances.

8. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business and record-keeping needs, and to meet any applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted or de-identified where appropriate and lawful.

9. Access and correction

You may contact us if you would like to request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

We may need to verify your identity before actioning a request.

10. Privacy questions or complaints

If you have a question or concern about how AssetTFM handles personal information, contact us through the General Enquiry form on this website.

Please select or state that your enquiry relates to privacy so it can be directed appropriately. We will take reasonable steps to review and respond to privacy concerns.

11. External links

This website may contain links to external services or websites. AssetTFM is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. You should review the privacy information provided by those services where relevant.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technology providers or information-handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page with the date of the latest update.
