Commercial Building Services

Responsive maintenance, fit-outs, refurbishments and project works for commercial properties.

Commercial building services

AssetTFM delivers commercial building services supported by more than 20 years of building and facilities management industry experience.

Our team coordinates maintenance, reactive works, refurbishments, fit-outs and project requirements with a focus on safety, professionalism, efficiency and minimising disruption to the property.

Commercial trade services

Our trade services can support one-off works, ongoing maintenance and larger projects. Services include, but are not limited to:

  • General building maintenance
  • Reactive repairs
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Commercial fit-outs
  • Electrical services
  • Plumbing services
  • Carpentry
  • Painting and plastering
  • Tiling and glazing
  • Flooring
  • Cabinetry
  • Signage installation
  • Window frosting and blinds
  • Concrete and resurfacing
  • Landscaping and irrigation

Building reporting

AssetTFM can inspect and report on maintenance issues, defects and longer-term asset requirements. Reports can help inform:

  • Repair priorities
  • Preventative maintenance planning
  • Insurance matters
  • Property management strategies
  • Commercial condition assessments

Project management

From scope development through to handover, AssetTFM can coordinate contractors, site activity, documentation and project delivery while keeping communication clear for the client.
