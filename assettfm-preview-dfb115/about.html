About AssetTFM

Practical building services backed by more than 20 years of building and facilities management industry experience.

Who we support

AssetTFM supports domestic, commercial, government, corporate, public and corporate housing and owners corporation clients with building maintenance, trade services, reporting and project management.

Our focus is straightforward: provide responsive service, practical solutions and reliable workmanship, supported by clear communication from the first enquiry through to completion.

How we work

One point of contact, broad capability

Building work often involves more than one trade, supplier or stage. AssetTFM coordinates the work required and keeps the client informed, helping projects and maintenance tasks move forward without unnecessary complexity.

Our work can include everyday maintenance, reactive repairs, planned improvements, fit-outs, refurbishments, building inspections and larger managed projects.

Experience

20+ years of building and facilities management industry experience

AssetTFM has worked across a wide range of property and building environments. That experience helps us understand the different priorities of homeowners, property managers, commercial operators, government facilities, housing providers and owners corporations.
