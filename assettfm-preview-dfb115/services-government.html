Government Building Services

Coordinated building services for government facilities and public environments.

Government building services

AssetTFM has experience working across government facilities and public-access environments, supporting maintenance, trade services, building reporting and project works.

We understand the importance of clear communication, coordinated site activity and practical delivery in facilities that may need to remain operational while work is underway.

Trade and maintenance services

Our trade services can support one-off works, ongoing maintenance and larger projects. Services include, but are not limited to:

  • General building maintenance
  • Reactive repairs
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Electrical and plumbing services
  • Carpentry
  • Painting and plastering
  • Flooring
  • Tiling and glazing
  • Signage
  • Window furnishings
  • External and grounds-related works
  • Refurbishment and improvement works

Reporting and inspections

Building reporting can help identify:

  • Maintenance issues
  • Defects
  • Repair priorities
  • Longer-term asset requirements
  • Condition considerations

Reports can include photographic records, recommendations and practical repair or maintenance advice.

Project works

AssetTFM can provide a single point of contact for planning, contractor coordination, site supervision, documentation and handover.
